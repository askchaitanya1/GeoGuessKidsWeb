Privacy Policy

🛡️ Short version: Geo Guess Kids does not collect, store, or share any personal information. No accounts. No tracking. No ads.

📱 About This App

Geo Guess Kids is a free visual learning app for children. It provides flashcards and quizzes across 15 categories — car logos, country flags, Pokémon, animals, dinosaurs, geography, shapes, colors, and more.

The app is available on Android and iOS and is designed for children aged 3–12, used under parental supervision.

📊 Data We Do NOT Collect

Geo Guess Kids does not collect any of the following:

  • Names, email addresses, or any personal identifiers
  • Location data
  • Photos, camera, or microphone access
  • Contacts or device files
  • Browsing history or usage analytics
  • Payment information

🔔 Push Notifications (Android)

The Android app uses Salesforce Marketing Cloud (SFMC) to send optional push notifications — such as learning reminders and parent messages.

To enable push notifications, the app assigns a randomly generated device identifier (Android ID) as a contact key in SFMC. This identifier:

  • Is not linked to any personal information
  • Is used solely to deliver push notifications to your device
  • Is not shared with any third parties beyond SFMC for the purpose of message delivery

You can disable push notifications at any time in your device's Settings → Apps → Geo Guess Kids → Notifications.

🌐 Images from Wikipedia

Some in-app images are loaded from Wikimedia Commons (upload.wikimedia.org) — a public repository of freely licensed media. No personal data is sent to Wikimedia when images load; it is a standard HTTPS image request.

👶 Children's Privacy (COPPA)

Geo Guess Kids is designed for children. We take children's privacy seriously and comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA).

We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children under 13. The app requires no account creation, no sign-in, and no input of personal details to use.

🔒 Data Security

Since we do not collect or store personal data, there is no personal data at risk. The app operates entirely on-device for all learning and quiz functionality.
