Built for Young Learners

Every design decision is made with kids in mind

🖼️
Real Photos & Maps
Wikipedia-sourced images for every item — not cartoon illustrations. Kids see the real world, not a simplified version of it.
🔊
Voice Pronunciation
Tap "Say it!" on any card and the app speaks the name aloud. Perfect for early readers or learning tricky pronunciations like Pterodactyl.
🗺️
Location Maps
USA States, India States, and Countries all show the location highlighted on a real world map — geography the way it should be learned.
🏆
Scored Quizzes
4-choice multiple choice quiz for every category. Score shown after each round with a fun emoji trophy based on how well you did.
📱
iOS & Android
Native app on both platforms. Fast Refresh means instant updates without a full install. Built with React Native and Expo SDK 54.
📬
Parent Inbox
Parents can send reminders and achievements directly to the app via Salesforce Marketing Cloud. No social media, no distractions.
